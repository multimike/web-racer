VILLAGE RACE — GAMEPLAY VISION

Final image: village-race-gameplay-vision.png (1672 × 941 pixels)
Created with the built-in image generation tool, using the project's existing design sheets as visual references. This is an assembled art-direction concept, not a captured frame from the game engine or a render of finished 3D models.

Direction: inviting sunny village circuit, refined stylized materials, clean chase-camera composition and restrained racing HUD. Reef Rocket is the player vehicle; Neon Wasp, Riot Ram and Comet Fox are ahead. The route connects warm dirt, non-directional irregular cobblestone and an asphalt garage apron. Village houses, garage, market, trees and lavender rocks carry through the established asset design language.
