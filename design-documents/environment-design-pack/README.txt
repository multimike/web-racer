ENVIRONMENT DESIGN PACK — v1.0

16 prop designs in four illustrated sheets:
  trees/         T01 Rally Oak, T02 Switchback Pine, T03 Turbo Palm
  vegetation/    V01 Puff Bush, V02 Ribbon Grass, V03 Fan Fern,
                 V04 Spike Rosette, V05 Coral Blooms
  large-rocks/   R01 Shoulder Boulder, R02 Split Fin, R03 Terrace Stack
  small-rocks/   S01 Pebble, S02 Wedge, S03 Flat Slab,
                 S04 Chip Cluster, S05 Broken Chunk

Open index.html for the illustrated catalog.
Each category contains design-sheet.png, model-specs.json, the exact generation prompt, and an individual text modeling brief for each asset.
STYLE-GUIDE.txt contains shared art direction and modeling conventions.
palette.json contains suggested sRGB colors.
asset-manifest.json lists the 16 assets and their dimensions.
references/original-buggies.png is the source style reference.

The sheets provide perspective and supplemental views. Trees have front/side references, large rocks have front/side/top references, and vegetation/small rocks have overhead references. Views are illustrative rather than exact CAD projections.
Dimensions are proposed starting points. Hidden geometry, game collision and final optimization must be resolved during 3D production.
No finished 3D meshes, rigs, UV textures or engine prefabs are included.

PROVENANCE
Created with the built-in image_gen tool. Exact prompts are saved per category.
The original buggy concept sheet established the style. Vegetation also used the new tree sheet; small rocks also used the large-rock sheet.
