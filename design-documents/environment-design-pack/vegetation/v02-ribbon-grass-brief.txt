V02 — RIBBON GRASS — MODELING BRIEF v1.0

REFERENCE
Use the matching labeled column of design-sheet.png and the shared STYLE-GUIDE.txt.
These are stylized concept references, not finished meshes or exact orthographic blueprints.

PROPOSED SIZE
Width X: 0.85 m
Depth Y: 0.75 m
Height Z: 0.65 m
Suggested art-direction bounds, not measurements of the image. All are visible dimensions above nominal ground; any buried skirt is extra.

SILHOUETTE
A fan of broad curved ribbon blades.
Nine to twelve blades with a slight central fold.

CONSTRUCTION
Separate parts: blade_01..12.
Use wide tapered strips with readable edges. Do not substitute many hair-thin blades. Fewer solid blades can suit this style better than dense alpha cards.
Unseen backs and undersides are artist-designed continuations. Do not leave holes visible from a low chase camera or during jumps.

PIVOT AND COORDINATES
At ground contact center, Z=0; root flare/rocks may extend a small skirt below ground.
+X right, +Y rearward from the front reference, +Z up; meters. Export conversion is engine-specific.
Front means looking toward +Y; this is an environment-prop convention, not the buggy's driving direction.

MATERIALS
Use palette groups: foliage, bark.
Suggested sRGB colors:
  foliage.jade: #139B68
  foliage.deep_teal: #116957
  foliage.lime: #9BD916
  foliage.highlight: #C7F039
  bark.base: #B86132
  bark.shadow: #87452B
  bark.highlight: #E8994A
All organic and stone materials: metallic 0, emission 0. Suggested roughness: foliage 0.65–0.80, bark 0.80–0.90, stone 0.80–0.95, petals 0.55–0.70.
Use broad color variation and restrained occlusion. Do not bake the sheet's studio highlights or shadows into albedo.

COLLISION
Nonblocking.

WIND / MOTION
Anchor every blade at the base; increasing bend toward tips.

VARIATION
Suggested uniform scale range: 0.75–1.25x.
Yaw: 0–360 degrees when placement allows.
These are suggested placement ranges. Keep silhouette proportions; do not freely randomize nonuniform scale. Check tree lean, rock notch direction, and camera visibility.
Keep original-sized master geometry before making variants.

MODELING CHECKS
1. Block out the bounding box and silhouette; compare with the 3.25–3.70 m long buggy concepts.
2. Resolve differences between the illustrative views into one coherent mesh.
3. Add broad facets, rounded bevels, thickness and a plausible backside.
4. Use shared material palettes. Set UV density and mesh budgets after platform and camera distance are known.
5. Check the chase-camera silhouette, ground contact, collision and any wind deformation in the actual game.
