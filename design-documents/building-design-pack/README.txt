references/ preserves the original buggy and rock style references.

Shacks and houses have perspective, front, side and rear views.
Services and landmarks add roof/top views.
These are modeling references with proposed dimensions, not finished meshes, exact CAD blueprints or construction documents. Images can disagree slightly across views; follow each written brief's resolutions.
