BUILDING STYLE GUIDE — ORIGINAL BUGGY WORLD

STYLE
A friendly village with colorful painted timber and plaster, chunky cream trim, broad roof seams and lavender-gray stone foundations. Match the original clean buggies and natural props. Buildings use simpler, quieter detail than the cars so racers remain legible.
The futuristic touch is restrained: small solar panels, rounded lamps and the garage's industrial forms. No ruins, wasteland armor or heavy rust.

MATERIAL TARGETS
palette.json provides proposed sRGB base colors.
Plaster: metallic 0, roughness 0.70–0.85.
Painted timber: metallic 0, roughness 0.60–0.80.
Painted roof metal: metallic 0 for the coating, roughness 0.35–0.50.
Exposed metal: metallic 1, roughness 0.35–0.50.
Stone: metallic 0, roughness 0.80–0.95.
Awning fabric: metallic 0, roughness 0.80.
Window glass/recesses: use simple dark teal material or shallow interior box; avoid realistic mirror reflections if they distract.
Keep emission only on lamps. Solar cells are dark blue, not glowing.

FORM AND DETAIL
Broad rounded edges, thick fascia, oversized readable handles and simple plank seams.
Suggested visible trim widths 0.12–0.20 m, important edge bevels 0.03–0.08 m. These are stylized art targets, not construction guidance.
Main roof overhangs typically 0.30 m. Use 0.50 m composition grid for main masses and 0.10 m grid for trim where convenient.
Do not force every architectural component onto a grid at the expense of the design.

BUILDING VARIANTS
Warm: cream walls, coral roof, teal frames.
Coastal: turquoise walls, cobalt roof, cream frames.
Sunny: mustard walls, teal roof, coral door.
Village houses can use these three paint combinations without changing topology.
Keep stone, timber and cream trim consistent across the village. Use brighter cobalt/orange mainly for the garage.

ORIENTATION
Meters, +Z up. Pivot at center of front wall at ground level. +Y extends from front facade into the building; +X is screen-right in front elevation. Front porches/stairs extend toward negative Y.
This is a building assembly convention. Convert to the target engine on export.
The main footprint excludes attachments. Check each brief's larger placement envelope. These are blockout allowances, not guaranteed clearance volumes.
