VILLAGE ASSEMBLY GUIDE

STARTER VILLAGE
Use B10 Village Hall at a central plaza, B08 Market Hall beside it, and B04–B06 houses around the outer edges.
Place B07 Race Garage near the course entrance with an open vehicle apron.
Use B01–B03 shacks on the village fringe, by a coastal path, or near service areas.
Place B09 Water Tower behind the rooftops as a navigation landmark.

SUGGESTED FIRST BLOCKOUT
A 60 × 60 m area is enough for a small initial composition; expand once racing speed and camera are known.
Start with 6–9 houses using two or three palette variants, 3–5 shacks, one garage, one market, one village hall and one water tower.
Try a central plaza about 18 × 18 m, with an 8–10 m clear racing street around or through it. These are game-design starting values, not tested track dimensions.
Reserve an apron at least 10 m wide × 6 m deep outside the garage; test turning with the actual buggy controller.
Avoid placing external stairs, stilt supports, awnings or balcony columns inside the drivable corridor.
Keep the inside of fast corners visually open. Taller landmarks help orientation without needing roadside clutter.

ENVIRONMENT CONNECTIONS
Use existing broadleaf trees in small courtyard groups; keep trunks outside the driving line.
Palms work well at the village edge near stilt huts. Pines suit the uphill approach.
Use bushes and flowers beside entrances, broad grass in quieter edges, and small rocks as nonblocking scatter.
Large boulders can define the outskirts. Do not hide hard obstacles behind foliage.

REPETITION WITHOUT UNIFORMITY
Vary paint regions before stretching geometry.
Rotate entire houses, swap shutter types, change optional lamps, and selectively add solar panels.
Mix the three house massings to avoid identical rooflines. Use taller Corner Houses sparingly near street corners.
Do not mirror a model without also moving its stairs, balcony access, chimney and door hinges consistently.

READABILITY AND MODELING SCOPE
Check the scene from a low chase camera and during jumps.
The sheets provide exterior concepts. They do not include an implemented level, furnished interiors, finished meshes or engine prefabs.
Keep the first village blockout simple; finalize scale, collision and camera clearances before detailed UVs and trim work.
