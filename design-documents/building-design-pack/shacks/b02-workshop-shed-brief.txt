DESIGN
Turquoise utility shed, mustard double doors, cobalt mono-pitch roof.
Use the matching labeled column of design-sheet.png. These are design references, not finished meshes or exact CAD drawings.

PROPOSED SCALE
Main body: 4 m wide X × 3.5 m deep Y.
Total height: 3.3 m.
Reserve at least 4.8 × 4.2 m for the building and attachments during blockout.
Proposed game-art dimensions, not measured CAD. Main body width/depth exclude attached stairs, balconies and eaves; total height includes roof/tower. Placement envelope is a blockout allowance for attachments, not a gameplay clearance or building code.
Building masses are shown at different display sizes on the sheets; do not infer relative size from pixels.

ROOF AND HEIGHTS
One planar roof: high along the left wall at 3.3 m, low along the right wall at 2.8 m; slope is across X only.

OPENING SCHEDULE
Front: centered double doors. Right side: one high window. Rear: one small vent. Left side: plain.
Left/right refer to the front elevation. Use shared nominal pedestrian doors 1.0 m wide × 2.2 m high unless noted otherwise. Window sizes are art targets; adapt within the proposed wall layout.

SEPARATE MODEL PARTS
wall_shell, stone_plinth, mono_pitch_roof, fascia, doors_pair, side_window, rear_vent, exterior_lamp, solar_panel

CONSTRUCTION
Double door opening 1.8 m wide by 2.2 m high. Treat this as pedestrian/storage access, not a buggy garage. Keep the roof panel flush.

RESOLVE THE VIEWS
The sheet's side and rear views disagree about roof slope. Use the single X-sloping plane specified here; do not slope the roof in both directions.
Some reference insets have perspective or small geometry discrepancies. Build one coherent model using this written brief for layout, the main image for styling, and the supporting views for further shape guidance.

ORIGIN AND ORIENTATION
At ground level, horizontally centered on the FRONT main-wall baseline. Main body occupies X=-width/2..+width/2 and Y=0..depth; front attachments extend into negative Y.
Meters, +X right in front view, +Y into building from front facade, +Z up.
Use hinge pivots for doors and shutters; keep source meshes at unscaled unit transforms before export.

MATERIALS
Follow palette.json and STYLE-GUIDE.txt. Separate wall, roof, trim, timber, stone, glass, lamp and optional decal regions. Keep medium-size bevels and readable trim. Avoid tiny plank grain or noisy plaster.
Matte plaster and timber, satin painted metal roofs, dark glass/recesses. Only actual lamps need emissive masks. No daytime neon outline around the building.

REUSE AND VARIATION
Keep openings and overall scale consistent. Swap predefined paint regions, shutters, roof accents and optional lamps. Mirror only when balcony/stair access and roof attachments are also resolved.
Use complete buildings as placement assets; reusable window, door, roof-trim and railing modules reduce modeling work. The illustrations do not establish a fully interchangeable wall-panel kit.

COLLISION AND INTERIORS
Use simple collision aligned with main walls. Doors and vehicle bays should remain clear only if the game allows entry.
Model shallow recess boxes for windows and non-enterable openings so the low chase camera cannot see through walls.
Close back faces, roof undersides and foundations visible during jumps. Detailed furnished interiors are outside this design pack.
For the garage, verify the bay opening with the widest buggy and use collision that respects the actual opening.

MODELING SEQUENCE
1. Block out the body, roof and attachments at the proposed dimensions.
2. Check with a buggy proxy: up to 3.70 m long, 2.25 m wide and roughly 2 m high.
3. Place openings from the schedule; resolve view inconsistencies before detailing.
4. Build trim, frames, roof sections and reusable parts. Close exposed undersides.
5. Apply palettes and restrained material variation. Tune budget, LODs and textures for the target game platform.
6. Review from the actual chase camera, around corners and during jumps.
